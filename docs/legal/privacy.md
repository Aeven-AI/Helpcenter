# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 23 October 2026**

Aeven is a desktop application, and it is built so that most of what you do with it never leaves your computer. This page says exactly what does leave it, why, and what you can do about that.

It covers the Aeven application, this help center, and the Aeven account behind them. It does not cover the services you connect to Aeven — mail, calendars, chat, code hosting — which keep their own policies.

## 1. Who is responsible

Aeven is provided by **[legal entity name]**, [registered address], the Netherlands ("Aeven", "we"). For anything on this page, write to [privacy@aeven.io](mailto:privacy@aeven.io).

## 2. What stays on your computer

These things are stored locally, in Aeven's own data folder on the machine where it runs, and are not uploaded to us:

- **Your files.** Aeven reads and writes the files in the folder you chose. They stay where they are.
- **Your conversations** with Aeven, unless a model needs to receive one to answer (see section 5).
- **Your settings**, including the folder you chose, your approval policy, and the standing instructions you wrote.
- **Your memory**, meaning the facts, preferences and learned procedures Aeven keeps across conversations, unless you chose to host that layer elsewhere.
- **Your sign-in state** for the account you signed in with.

You can delete any of it by deleting the relevant item in the app, or by removing Aeven's data folder. [Your data](/docs/account/your-data) lists where each thing lives.

## 3. What we hold on our side

| What | Why | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account identity | To sign you in and keep your plan attached to you | The name, email address and provider account ID returned by Google, Apple or X when you sign in |
| Plan and credits | To meter usage and bill correctly | Current plan, credit balance, top-ups, adjustments |
| Payment records | Because invoices and tax records are the law | Amounts, dates, invoices, last four digits of a card; card numbers are handled by our payment provider, not by us |
| Technical logs | To keep the service working and secure | App version, platform, error reports, request times, coarse location derived from IP |
| Support conversations | To answer you | The messages you send to support |

We do not buy personal data about you from anyone, and we do not sell or rent what we hold.

## 4. Why we are allowed to process it

Under the GDPR we rely on:

- **Performance of a contract** — the account, plan, credits and billing that make the service work.
- **Legitimate interests** — keeping the service secure, diagnosing faults, preventing abuse, and improving how it behaves. We balance those interests against your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — invoices, tax, and responding to lawful requests.
- **Consent** — where we ask for something beyond the above, such as optional product email. You can withdraw it at any time.

## 5. What happens when a model answers

To produce an answer, the content of a task — your message, the parts of your files Aeven read for it, the images you attached, and the relevant conversation history — is sent to the model that is answering.

- **Aeven models.** The task goes to the model infrastructure we operate with our processors, under a data processing agreement that forbids using it to train models for anyone else.
- **Your own provider.** If you connected one, the task goes to that provider directly, under their terms and their privacy policy. You chose that; we do not sit in the middle.

We do not use your content to train models. Where a model provider is involved that is their commitment to us as well, and it is written into our agreements with them.

Changing which model answers does not move your memory, conversations or connections. [Choose a model](/docs/using/models) explains the choice.

## 6. Connected accounts

When you connect Gmail, Google Drive, Outlook, Slack, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, GitHub or another service, the provider issues a token that Aeven uses to act on your behalf. That token is stored by Aeven for as long as the connection exists.

- We do not ask for your passwords to those services.
- Removing a connection in **Settings → Integrations** deletes the token on our side; revoking Aeven in the provider's own settings does the same from their side.
- What Aeven may read or write in a connected service is limited by the scopes you granted when you connected it.

## 7. Who else sees your data

We share personal data only with:

- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** that run our account service, in the EU or under EU-approved safeguards.
- **Our payment provider**, for subscriptions, credits and invoices.
- **Model providers**, for the tasks that go to them, as described in section 5.
- **Email and support tools**, when you write to us.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it and the request is valid.

A current list of processors is available on request from [privacy@aeven.io](mailto:privacy@aeven.io). Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it does so under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

## 8. How long we keep things

| Thing | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Account and plan | While your account exists |
| Payment and invoice records | Seven years, as Dutch tax law requires |
| Technical logs | 30 days, unless needed to investigate a security incident |
| Support conversations | Two years after the last message |
| Anything on your computer | Until you delete it — we have no copy |

Deleting your account removes the account data we hold. Files and conversations on your computer stay there, because they were never ours.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to see what we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent you gave. Write to [privacy@aeven.io](mailto:privacy@aeven.io); we answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the **Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens**, or to the supervisory authority where you live.

## 10. Security

Traffic between the app and our services is encrypted in transit. Account data at rest is encrypted by our hosting providers. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to run the service, and is logged.

No system is perfect. If you find a vulnerability, report it to [security@aeven.io](mailto:security@aeven.io) rather than publishing it, and we will work with you.

Because Aeven is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence, you can also read the code and check what it does with your files: [github.com/Aeven-AI/Aeven](https://github.com/Aeven-AI/Aeven).

## 11. Children

Aeven is not for people under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them; if you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

## 12. Cookies and this help center

This site is a static documentation site. It sets no advertising cookies and runs no analytics that identify you. The only thing it stores in your browser is your light or dark preference and your search index in memory. [aeven.io](https://aeven.io) has its own, separate notice.

## 13. Changes to this policy

When something material changes, we change the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
