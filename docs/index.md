# Help center

Aeven is an AI that works on your computer and in your connected apps. You describe what you want; Aeven works out the steps, does them, and asks you before anything that needs your say-so.

These pages are written for using Aeven, not for building with it. Every page starts from the screen you are on and ends with something done.

## Where to start

- **New here?** [Install Aeven and sign in](/docs/getting-started/install-and-sign-in), then [finish a first task](/docs/getting-started/your-first-task).
- **Wondering why this one is different?** [Aeven runs on your computer, keeps what it learns in a place you own, never reads your memory, and is open source](/docs/getting-started/why-aeven).
- **Using it daily?** Learn [how approvals work](/docs/using/approvals) and [where your work lands](/docs/using/where-your-work-lands).
- **Want it to run on its own?** Set up a [bot](/docs/automation/automations).
- **Bringing in mail, calendar, or chat?** Connect it under [Connectors](/docs/connections/connected-apps).
- **Teaching it a job once?** [Skills](/docs/using/skills) hold the procedure; a [bot](/docs/automation/automations) runs it on a schedule.
- **Which model answers?** The [Aeven models](/docs/using/models) are included and ready; your own key works too.
- **Something not working?** [Fix a problem](/docs/help/troubleshooting).
