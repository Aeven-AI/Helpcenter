# Slides and presentations

Aeven makes presentations with you, in a design you pick. Open **Inspiration** in the sidebar and choose the **Slides** tab, or ask for a deck in any conversation.

## Pick a template

The **Slides** tab shows 36 slide templates: Dark Pills, Bauhaus Mono, Lush Lux, Startup Pitch, Magazine, Pixel Quest and more. Click one to page through all of its slides. Every template has the same kinds of slides (title, agenda, key points, numbers, timeline, quote, chart, comparison, team, thank you), so any presentation fits any template.

## Start the presentation

- Press **Use template** and finish the sentence in the composer with what the presentation is about, then send it.
- Or start from what you already have: under **Start from your own material**, **Add a file** (PowerPoint, Keynote, Pages, PDF or Word). Aeven keeps its content and story and rebuilds it in the template's design.

No template in mind? Type in the **Slides** tab's composer (**"Describe the presentation you need"**), or ask in any conversation: *"make a ten-slide deck from this report"*.

## Aeven builds it with you

Aeven first asks a few questions (who it is for, how long it should be, the one thing people should remember), proposes an outline, and then builds the slides in exactly the template's design. The template's sample slides are a guide, not the finished deck: your content replaces them, and Aeven adds what your story needs, such as charts drawn from your numbers, diagrams, comparisons, your own photos and short videos.

Change anything by asking: *"split slide 4 in two"*, *"make the chart a line chart"*, *"use the Magazine template instead"*. Switching templates keeps every word.

## Downloads

The files are saved in a **presentations** folder in your workspace.

| Format | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| PDF | Every slide as one page, exactly as designed |
| PowerPoint | Editable text, shapes and pictures in the template's fonts, with your speaker notes. The fonts travel inside the file, so the deck looks the same on a computer without them |
| Keynote | Made from the PowerPoint file by Keynote, on a Mac with the desktop app and Keynote installed |
| HTML | The deck for presenting from the browser, with its animations and video |

Aeven asks for your approval before it makes the Keynote file, because it installs the template's fonts on your Mac and opens Keynote to convert the deck. A Keynote file does not carry its fonts: to open it on another Mac, install the template's fonts there. They are all free on Google Fonts.

## What it costs

A presentation spends credits from your plan like any other request. Longer decks, and pictures you ask Aeven to generate for them, cost more. [Account, plans and credits](/docs/account/plans-and-credits)
