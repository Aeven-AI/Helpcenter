# Images and video

Aeven makes images and short videos. Ask for one in a conversation, or open **Inspiration** in the sidebar and start from a ready-made idea; either way the result arrives in the conversation, where you can ask for changes.

## Asking in a conversation

Describe what you want the way you would describe it to a person: the subject, the light, the framing, the mood, the words on it. The more concrete the description, the closer the first attempt lands.

Vague: *"a nice picture for my presentation"*.
Concrete: *"a wide photo of a bright meeting room in the morning, big windows, one notebook on the table, soft shadows, no people, space on the left for a title."*

## Inspiration

**Inspiration** in the sidebar opens on its **Image & video** tab:

- A composer for **"Describe a new image or video"**: type what you want and send it.
- **Image ideas**: retouch a phone photo into a polished post, a watercolour, a studio product shot, a sticker set, an anime illustration, a retro poster, claymation, pixel art, a new hairstyle, and short video shots. Click one to see a **Before** and **After**, add **Your photo** when the idea starts from one, and pick an **Aspect ratio** (**Auto** matches your photo). **Try it** puts the complete prompt in the composer, where you can still change it before you send it.

The **Slides** tab next to it holds the presentation templates. [Slides and presentations](/docs/using/slides)

## Editing an image you already have

Attach the picture and say what to change. Aeven treats it as the reference: *"same person, same pose, warmer light, cleaner background"* keeps what you did not ask about and changes only what you did. Say **same face** or **keep the framing** when that matters; a photo retouch goes wrong when a model redraws the scene instead of finishing it.

## Video

Video works the same way: describe the shot, the camera movement and how long it should be, and the clip arrives in the conversation. A clip takes longer than an image, so Aeven keeps working while you carry on — the [jobs list](/docs/using/background-jobs) shows it running.

## Changing the result

Say what is wrong, not that it is wrong: *"the second one, but with less clutter and the logo smaller"*. Each round is a new file, so nothing you liked is overwritten — the earlier versions stay in the conversation.

## What it costs

Images and video are made by a model, and each one spends credits from your plan like any other request. Longer clips and bigger images cost more. [Account, plans and credits](/docs/account/plans-and-credits)
