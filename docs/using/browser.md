# The browser

Æven can open websites, read them, and click and type in them for you. You watch it happen: a pointer moves to each link, button and field before Æven acts.

## In the sidebar

Ask Æven to open a site — "open linkedin", "find the opening hours on their website" — and it opens in the **Browser** in the right sidebar. Each **Browser** pill is a tab; Æven can open more than one and switches between them with its pointer.

- The toolbar works for you and for Æven: back, forward, reload, copy a screenshot, and the **More** menu for zoom, appearance, and opening the page in Chrome.
- When a step is yours — signing in, a captcha, confirming a payment — Æven shows a short message saying what to do and waits for you. It never types your passwords.
- Æven asks before it reads your clipboard, searches the pages you visited, clears cookies, or uses tools a site offers to assistants.

## In your own browser

The **Æven extension** lets Æven work in the browser you already use, with the sites you are already signed in to.

1. Install **Æven** from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cpgmlafibkmklfnpjpjbdgoegpljfofa) in Chrome, Brave or Edge.
2. Keep that browser open while you use Æven. **Settings → General → Browser extension** says which browsers are connected.

Then ask Æven to do something "in my browser". It opens its own tabs in an **Æven** tab group, in front, so you can watch, and works only in those tabs; your other tabs are never read. With your approval it can also search your browsing history and bookmarks.

While Æven acts in a tab, the browser shows "Æven started debugging this browser". That is the browser telling you an extension is controlling a tab; the bar goes away a few seconds after Æven stops.

| Where you use Æven | How the extension connects |
| --- | --- |
| The desktop app | Through the app on the same computer. Several browsers can be connected at once, and Æven opens the browser itself when it needs it and none is running. |
| Æven Web | Through the [chat.aeven.io](https://chat.aeven.io) page you have open, in the same browser. |

On Æven Web the extension is the only browser Æven has, so "open linkedin" opens the site in a new tab of your browser. If the extension is missing when Æven needs it, a window titled **Let Æven use your browser** offers **Add to Chrome** (Edge and Brave show their own name). Add the extension in the tab that opens and come back: Æven notices it by itself and carries on, without reloading the page. If you took longer than Æven waited, the window shows **Continue** once the extension is added. **Not now** lets Æven finish without your browser. Safari, Firefox and phone browsers cannot add the extension; open Æven Web in Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer, or use the desktop app.

Choosing **Browser** in the right sidebar on Æven Web opens a new tab of your browser through the extension, in Æven's tab group. Without the extension, it offers to continue in the desktop app instead. [Your desktop from Æven Web](/docs/using/desktop-from-web)
