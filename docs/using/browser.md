# The browser

Aeven can open websites, read them, and click and type in them for you. You watch it happen: a pointer moves to each link, button and field before Aeven acts.

## In the sidebar

Ask Aeven to open a site — "open linkedin", "find the opening hours on their website" — and it opens in the **Browser** in the right sidebar. Each **Browser** pill is a tab; Aeven can open more than one and switches between them with its pointer.

- The toolbar works for you and for Aeven: back, forward, reload, copy a screenshot, and the **More** menu for zoom, appearance, and opening the page in Chrome.
- When a step is yours — signing in, a captcha, confirming a payment — Aeven shows a short message saying what to do and waits for you. It never types your passwords.
- Aeven asks before it reads your clipboard, searches the pages you visited, clears cookies, or uses tools a site offers to assistants.

## In your own browser

The **Aeven extension** lets Aeven work in the browser you already use, with the sites you are already signed in to.

1. Install **Aeven** from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cpgmlafibkmklfnpjpjbdgoegpljfofa) in Chrome, Brave or Edge.
2. Keep that browser open while you use Aeven. **Settings → General → Browser extension** says which browsers are connected.

Then ask Aeven to do something "in my browser". It opens its own tabs in an **Aeven** tab group, in front, so you can watch, and works only in those tabs; your other tabs are never read. With your approval it can also search your browsing history and bookmarks.

While Aeven acts in a tab, the browser shows "Aeven started debugging this browser". That is the browser telling you an extension is controlling a tab; the bar goes away a few seconds after Aeven stops.

| Where you use Aeven | How the extension connects |
| --- | --- |
| The desktop app | Through the app on the same computer. Several browsers can be connected at once, and Aeven opens the browser itself when it needs it and none is running. |
| Aeven Web | Through the [chat.aeven.io](https://chat.aeven.io) page you have open, in the same browser. |
