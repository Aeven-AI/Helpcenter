# Your desktop from Æven Web

[chat.aeven.io](https://chat.aeven.io) works on our servers, in a sandbox. Keep the Æven desktop app open and signed in, and a conversation you type on the web runs on your computer instead: Æven can use your folders, your apps, computer use and the browser beside the conversation, from any browser, a phone included. There is nothing to set up. It is what the Æ in Æven also stands for: **Almost Everywhere**.

## What runs where

| Situation | Where Æven answers |
| --- | --- |
| A new conversation, and a desktop of yours is on | The desktop you used most recently |
| A conversation that last ran on a desktop that is on | That desktop |
| No desktop is on | Æven Cloud, the sandbox on our servers |
| The desktop does not take the message within about ten seconds | Æven Cloud, so you never wait for nothing |

The answer appears in the web page as it is written, the same as on the desktop. Files Æven makes stay on the computer that made them.

## Choosing a desktop

Choose **Options** at the top of the conversation, then **Choose desktop**. The list shows each desktop of your account, whether it is **Online** or when it was last seen, and a monitor mark on the one the conversation runs on.

- **Press a desktop** to run the conversation's next messages there.
- **Press the marked desktop again** to deselect it: on the web the conversation runs in Æven Cloud again.
- **An offline desktop** cannot be chosen. Pressing it explains that you can choose it once it is on, and that until then the conversation continues in Æven Cloud.

None of this is available while Æven is answering; choose once the answer is done.

## From the desktop app

With more than one desktop on your account, the desktop app's **Options** has **Choose desktop** too. **This computer** is first in the list; the other desktops follow. Choose another desktop and the conversation runs there from every device — this computer, the web and your other desktops — while that desktop is on. Choose **This computer** to bring it back.

## Turning a desktop off

Quit the desktop app and it shows as offline at once, so new conversations go to Æven Cloud straight away. A desktop that loses its connection, or a laptop that goes to sleep, shows as offline within about two and a half minutes. An older version of the desktop app does not report that it is on and always shows as offline; update it to choose it.

## Desktops on the same network

Two desktops of your account on the same network, or on the same Tailscale network, connect to each other directly. Conversations, settings and messages then reach the other desktop without going through the internet, which is faster and keeps working while the internet is down. Each desktop proves it belongs to your account before it connects.
