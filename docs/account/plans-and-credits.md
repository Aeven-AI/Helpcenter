# Plans and credits

Your account page shows what you are on and what you have used. Nothing in it is hidden behind a support request.

## Your plan

**Account** shows the current plan, what it includes, and when it renews. Changing plan opens a checkout page in your browser; the account page updates when the payment finishes.

## Credits

Usage is metered in credits. The account page shows the balance, what has been spent recently, and the lines that make up the balance:

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Subscription | Credits included with the current plan |
| Top-up | Credits you added |
| Adjustment | A correction, such as a refund or a credited amount |

To add credits, choose an amount on the account page and complete the checkout. The balance updates as soon as the payment clears.

## When you run out

A task that needs more credits than are left stops and says so. Adding credits or moving to a plan that includes more is enough; the work continues from the same conversation.

## Payment history and invoices

The account page lists payments you have made. For an invoice or a VAT detail, the billing portal linked there is the place that holds them.
