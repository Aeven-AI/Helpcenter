# Account, plans and credits

Your account page is the single place that answers "what am I on, what have I used, what do I still have". Nothing in it needs a support request.

## Your account

**Account** — from your avatar in the corner — holds your name and photo, your language, your plan, your credit balance, this period's usage, billing, two-factor authentication, and **Sign out**.

Signing in with the same account on a second computer brings your memory, conversations and settings with it. [Your data](/docs/account/your-data) says where those copies live.

## Plans

| Plan | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Free | A preview allowance, enough to use Aeven properly before paying for it |
| Pro | A monthly allowance and the Aeven Flash and Pro models — the plan most people want |
| Heavy | Several times Pro's allowance, for work that runs all day |

The account page shows the exact figures for your account, what the current plan includes, and whether it renews or ends — with the date. **Upgrade** and **Downgrade** sit next to the plan you are on.

A plan that is not open for subscription yet says so; until it opens, credits carry you.

## Changing plan

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Upgrade | Applies at once. You pay the prorated difference today and the larger allowance is available immediately |
| Downgrade | Scheduled for renewal. You keep your current plan and allowance until then |
| Cancel | You keep the plan until the period ends, then move to Free |

Checkout opens in your browser; the account page updates itself when the payment finishes, so there is nothing to reload or confirm.

If a payment fails, a banner says so and asks you to update the payment method — the plan stays as it is while you do.

## Credits

Usage is metered in credits: a request spends them in proportion to the model that answers and how much work it does.

- **Your plan's allowance comes first.** Credits are spent once the allowance for the period is used up.
- **Credits never expire.** What you add stays on your account until it is spent.
- **Add credits any time** from the account page: choose an amount or enter your own, pay through Stripe, and the balance updates as soon as the payment clears.
- Top-ups are not available on every deployment; where they are not, the page says so instead of offering a checkout.

When a period's allowance is nearly used, Aeven says so once and points at both ways forward: a bigger plan, or more credits.

## Usage

The usage section covers the current period — the dates, the share of the allowance spent, and where it went. That is the honest answer to "why did this month cost more": a long task on a big model is not the same as a short question on a small one. [Choose a model](/docs/using/models)

## Billing and invoices

**Manage billing** opens the billing portal: payment method, past invoices, receipts and VAT details all live there, and changing a card happens there rather than in Aeven.

## When you run out

A task that needs more credits than are left stops and tells you. Add credits or move to a plan that includes more, then continue in the same conversation — nothing already finished is redone.

## Security

Two-factor authentication uses an authenticator app: scan the code, confirm one six-digit code, and keep the recovery codes it shows you — each works once, and they are the way back in if you lose the app. Turning it off asks for a code too, so a stolen session cannot quietly remove it.
